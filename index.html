Embodiment Practice Log

Saved only in this browser. To use another device, export and import on the Data tab.

Your practice, written down

A private log for your embodiment practices — yoga, meditation, breathwork, or anything else you add. It keeps a simple record of what you did, when, and how it felt.

How to use it

  1. Practices — add the practices you do, by name.
  2. Log — pick a practice, the day, and the part of the day. Add notes on what was present: feelings, sensations, whatever you want to remember. Notes are optional.
  3. Dashboard — see how often you practise, how your sessions spread across morning, afternoon and evening, and what the last 30 days look like.
  4. Data — download a backup, or move your log to another device.

Where your log lives

Everything stays in the browser you are using. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can read it. That also means your phone and your computer each keep their own log, so use Export and Import on the Data tab to move entries across and to keep a backup.

Import a log file

Adds the entries from an export file to this browser. Nothing already here is replaced or deleted, and entries you already have are skipped.

A session or practice you deleted here comes back, with its sessions, if the file you import still contains it.
